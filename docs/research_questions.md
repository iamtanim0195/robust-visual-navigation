# Research Questions

## Main Research Question
How does multi-sensor fusion of vision, wheel odometry, and inertial measurements affect the accuracy and robustness of autonomous navigation in a low-cost mobile robot under visual degradation?

## Sub-Research Questions
- **RQ1**: How does sensor fusion affect localization accuracy (Absolute Trajectory Error) compared with vision-only or wheel-odometry-based localization under nominal visual conditions?
- **RQ2**: How does visual degradation (blur, reduced resolution, low illumination) quantitatively impact autonomous navigation success rate, collision rate, and path efficiency?
- **RQ3**: Does fusing camera information, wheel odometry, and IMU measurements maintain state estimation stability when visual perception degrades severe levels?
- **RQ4**: How does navigation performance differ between Gazebo simulation and a physical low-cost robot (Sim-to-Real transfer gap)?
- **RQ5**: What accuracy-versus-computational-cost trade-offs exist across different sensor configurations on constrained compute nodes?
